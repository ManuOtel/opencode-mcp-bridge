# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in
this project a safe experience for every person.

We pledge to act in ways that support an open and welcoming community.
We welcome every person without regard to age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, skin color, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes the following actions:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different views and different experience.
- Accept constructive feedback with care.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show care toward other members of the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes the following actions:

- Use sexual language or imagery, or give unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insult or attack other persons, or make political comments about them.
- Harass any person in public or in private.
- Publish private information of other persons without clear permission.
- Act in any other way that is not fit for a shared project.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The maintainer reviews reports of unacceptable behavior.
The maintainer can remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not fit
this Code of Conduct.
The maintainer can remove any person from the project for behavior that
harms the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces.
It also applies when a person represents the project in public spaces.
Project spaces include issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews in
the `ManuOtel/opencode-mcp-bridge` repository.

## Enforcement

Report an incident to manuotel@gmail.com.
This address is the commercial-license contact in `LICENSE.md` and
`README.md`, and it also receives conduct reports.
Do not open a public issue for a conduct report.

Include the following facts in the report:

- Your contact address for follow-up questions.
- Names of the persons involved, if known.
- Time and place of the incident.
- A short description of the incident.
- Links or copies of public text, if available.

The maintainer will confirm receipt of the report.
The maintainer will examine the facts and decide on a response.
The maintainer will not share the reporter identity without permission.
Response timing is not guaranteed.
The project has no paid moderation team and no fixed SLA.

Enforcement can include the following steps:

1. A private warning with a request to stop the behavior.
2. A temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
3. Removal of contributions that contain the unacceptable behavior.
